## Mathematical safeguards

- For a right prism: lateral area = P × H; closed total area = P × H + 2B. H is the distance between the two chosen parallel bases, even when drawn diagonally or horizontally. It is distinct from an altitude used to calculate B.
- Trace every segment of a concave base boundary. Interior decomposition lines are not perimeter segments.
- Revision Q27 has a through-hole: include both outer and inner boundary lengths for the vertical surfaces, and subtract the hole from each base area.
- An open box has missing faces. If the omitted face is a base, use PH + B; if it is a lateral face (e.g. the sloping opening of the file holder), omit that rectangle instead. Do not infer the missing face from the phrase "without a lid" alone.
- Revision Q31 concerns the inner bottom and wetted portions of four walls, using water depth. Do not count the water's free surface as tank material.
- Revision Q33 is a composite solid. Add the three cube surface areas, then subtract both copies of each contact patch: 6(3² + 4² + 5²) − 2(3² + 4²) = 250 cm².
- Preserve supplied rounded base areas. Exercise 9B Q16 gives B = 3.9 cm²; Consolidation Q1 gives B = 127 mm². Do not silently replace them with trigonometric exact values from an ideal drawing.
- The chapter distinguishes polygonal prisms from cylinders. A cylinder extension is outside the first build.

## Exclusions checked

- Exercise 9A is planar area only.
- Exercise 9B Q7, 9–10, 13–15, 29–30, 32–34 are volume/capacity/recasting/water questions without a surface-area part. Q33 shows a net but asks about capacity only; it can be a later optional net activity.
- Revision Q1–9 and 19–23 are planar-area questions; Q16–18, 28, 32, 34–36 are volume/depth questions.
- Challenge Questions on 9.44 are volume/water displacement. MC Q9–10 (9.46), other Exam-type and Public Exam questions do not ask for surface area.
- Check Yourself Q1–2 support identifying right prisms and bases, so may be used as short prerequisite prompts, but are excluded from the 54-entry surface-area count.
