# Surface Area Studio — full chapter bank

User authorised the full rollout on 23/10/2026, retaining unfolding and image export. Earlier instruction to use judgement and skip further design questions still applies. This extends the approved design's Phase 2; no deployment or new dependencies.

## Design and scope

- Include the audited 54 numbered questions, five worked examples (including the existing pilot), and Activity 9.3: 60 bank entries. Multiple solids belong to variants within their numbered entry.
- Search and section filters, source page references and direct links identify presets. Selection resets the teaching state and hides working.
- Store source givens, actual geometry, open/missing surfaces, authored calculations and independently checked expectations separately. Unknown labels remain symbolic until the final working is revealed.
- Generalise rigid hinge unfolding to polygon boundaries, concave bases and inner boundaries. Show missing lateral faces as gaps; count only existing bases. Water examples show submerged surfaces or the added water band, as appropriate.
- Joined cubes use a separate exposed-face/separation view. Never describe the composite as one PH prism.
- Keep current-stage and comparison PNG/SVG exports, clean view, projection, mouse/touch/keyboard controls and hidden-answer behaviour.
